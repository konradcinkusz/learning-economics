\section*{The year at a glance}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}

\toprule
\textbf{Part} & \textbf{Days} & \textbf{Season} & \textbf{Money} & \textbf{The ideas} \\
\midrule
1 & 1--65 & autumn & up to €20 & The value of things, bartering and

2 & 66--130 & winter & up to €50 & Choosing; the money of long ago; who

4 & 196--260 & summer & up to €100 & First things first; comparing

\bottomrule

\vspace{6mm}
Each part has 13 weeks of 5 days -- including the holidays: the book is
still a page a day from Monday to Friday at Christmas and in the summer,
like \emph{First Words}. Each week has one idea and one word, and the
money grows through the year: up to €20 in the autumn, up to €50 in the
winter and up to €100 in the spring and the summer, always in whole
euros.
